FAQ

Quick answers

How does book lending work?

Browse the Book Exchange, open a book and send a request to its owner. Agree on the details directly and return the book when you're done.

Can I ask for a book that isn't listed?

Yes. Use "List a book" and choose the Request type — other students can reach out if they have a copy.

Does it cost anything?

BookPortal is about sharing, so books are available at low or no cost.

How do I remove a book I listed?

Your listings are tagged "Yours" on the exchange page — use the bin button on the card to remove one.
